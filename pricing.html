Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my business listed?
Simply choose a plan and contact us — we'll handle the rest, including adding your photo, description, and map pin.
Can I choose which maps I appear on?
Yes! For the Single Map and Multi-Map tiers, you pick the specific neighborhood maps that best match your business location and audience.
What does "priority positioning" mean?
Multi-Map and All Maps listings appear at the top of their category section on both the interactive and print maps, giving them maximum visibility.
Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?
Listings are billed annually with no long-term contract. You can cancel before your renewal date and your listing will remain through the end of your paid period.
How many maps are currently available?
We currently cover neighborhoods across Charlotte, NC and Fort Mill, SC — with new areas added regularly. All Maps subscribers are automatically included on every new map at no extra cost.
